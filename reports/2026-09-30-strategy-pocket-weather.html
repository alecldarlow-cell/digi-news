TAKE YOUR TIME

A break in
the weather.

Controls: put your thumb anywhere in the valley and drag to steer. A small joystick follows your thumb; drag farther to move faster. Ease back into the small centre ring to stop — rain and sun only fall once you have stopped, so no water is wasted on the way.
Wind: blows while you steer. Sweep it over the mill to turn it, or over wet ground to dry it. Move through the cloud to carry it along. Release to let weather fade. Tap Ⅱ to pause.
Direct touch (beta, in settings): the weather sits just above your finger and glides after it. Hold still to rain or shine; a quick tap sends the cloud there.

Weather fades for 3 seconds after release. The cloud stays. About 5 seconds of sun stores enough warmth for watered crops to finish growing while you move on. Extra sun still dries the soil. The mill coasts after a gust. Its grain store holds 6 wheat; ripe crops wait safely until there is room.
Three stars: complete the harvest; keep peak flooding below 25; waste no more than 12% of your rain (at least 5 units allowed). Mill half the harvest in a lost round for a one-off consolation star.
From valley 2, a storm front rolls in after about a minute and rains wherever it drifts. Sweep Wind through it to drag it away, or hold Wind on it to break it up. In the last 30 seconds every harvest counts double.

Keyboard: 1/2/3 select weather. Focus the valley, hold arrows to steer and Space to apply weather. Escape pauses.
